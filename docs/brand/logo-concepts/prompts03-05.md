# Logo concepts 03–05: prompts and rationale

These are independent raster logo explorations for ネコネコインダストリー / NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES. NEKOMO remains the vehicle-toy product brand. The concepts use three different visual languages; none is selected here.

## 03 — Modular play

**Rationale:** Rounded, interlocking letter pieces connect the workshop to playful 3D-printed toys while keeping the company name as the mark.

**Colors:** Cobalt blue, warm yellow, vermilion red, white.

**Uses:** Toy packaging, workshop labels, and child-facing concept material.

**Final prompt:**

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: standalone landscape logo concept, about 3:2, generous white margins
Primary request: A colorful, playful modular identity for a cat-run workshop making 3D-printed vehicle toys, expressed only as a polished wordmark and small type.
Subject: The lowercase wordmark "nekoneko" built from original interlocking toy-block-like geometric rounded letter pieces, with one tiny integrated cat-ear detail. Keep every letter clearly readable. Place the exact small caption "INDUSTRIES" below.
Style/medium: Flat, crisp, premium children's design museum identity; simple vector-friendly raster logo concept.
Composition/framing: One centered horizontal standalone wordmark lockup on plain white, ample whitespace, landscape composition.
Color palette: Cobalt blue, warm yellow, and vermilion red with white negative space.
Text (verbatim): "nekoneko" and "INDUSTRIES"; spell exactly, no other text.
Constraints: Flat solid colors only, no gradients, no shadows, no border, no mockup. Distinctive, refined, professional typography; maintain excellent legibility.
Avoid: Literal vehicles, gears, generic clip-art, extra icons, slogans, watermark, existing trademarks.
```

## 04 — Heavy industry

**Rationale:** A compact shield and double chevron make the company read as a serious manufacturer; the paired points carry a subtle cat-ear cue.

**Colors:** Black, white, safety yellow.

**Uses:** Tooling labels, equipment, workshop signage, and monochrome-capable brand applications.

**Final prompt:**

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: standalone landscape logo concept, about 3:2, generous warm-white margins
Primary request: A strong, utilitarian manufacturer insignia for a cat-run workshop making 3D-printed vehicle toys.
Subject: A compact angular double-chevron mark that subtly suggests two cat ears, contained inside a simple hexagonal shield. Beside it, exact uppercase typography reading "NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES" in a wide, low lockup. Make the company name dominant and fully legible.
Style/medium: Serious industrial equipment badge aesthetic; precise engineered geometry and stencil-inspired typography, clean and professional.
Composition/framing: One centered horizontal standalone logo on a plain warm-white field, wide low proportions with ample whitespace.
Color palette: Black and white with safety yellow as the sole accent.
Text (verbatim): "NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES"; spell exactly, no other text.
Constraints: Crisp flat solid colors, no gradients, no shadows, no border, no mockup. Keep strong contrast and legibility at small scale.
Avoid: Kawaii face, toy-block shapes, colorful children's palette, gears, literal vehicles, slogans, watermark, existing trademarks.
```

## 05 — Japanese seal

**Rationale:** A vermilion craft stamp links the cat-run workshop to hands-on making; paired silhouettes and stacked lines hint at both cats and additive printing.

**Colors:** Vermilion red, warm white.

**Uses:** Craft-style labels, maker notes, packaging accents, and editorial identity material.

**Final prompt:**

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: standalone landscape logo concept, about 3:2, generous warm-white margins
Primary request: A contemporary Japanese craft seal identity for a cat-run workshop making 3D-printed vehicle toys.
Subject: A distinctive near-square vermilion seal mark with two abstract paired cat silhouettes and a subtle layered-line motif suggesting 3D printing. Place the emblem large above a restrained exact uppercase wordmark, "NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES". Use no Japanese lettering.
Style/medium: Calm wabi-sabi meets modern editorial identity; iconic, handmade character, simple enough to reproduce as a stamp.
Composition/framing: One centered vertical emblem-and-wordmark lockup, isolated on plain warm white, landscape canvas with ample clear whitespace.
Color palette: Vermilion red only on warm white, with a small amount of white negative space inside the seal.
Materials/textures: Mostly clean flat vermilion shapes, with a very subtle irregular ink edge around the square seal; keep all cat forms and layered lines crisp.
Text (verbatim): "NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES"; spell exactly, no other text.
Constraints: Professional, balanced, legible, original. No gradients, no shadows, no border, no mockup.
Avoid: Industrial shield, gears, colorful toy blocks, literal vehicles, cute cartoon face, extra Japanese text, slogans, watermark, existing trademarks.
```
