共通意図: NEKONEKO INDUSTRIESが作る、携帯できる子ども向け玩具NEKOMOのロゴ案。車、船、飛行機の3種類を各案の中でそれぞれ認識できるようにする。黒と白だけを使った、簡潔なロゴを提案する。

共通表記: 会社名は `NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES`、ミッションは `Wonder to Go.` と正確に記す。マークを主役にし、会社名をその下、ミッションをさらに小さく配置する。白地の横3:2、広い余白、フラットなロゴ表現。

最終プロンプト:

```text

Asset type: original company logo concept, vector-friendly
Primary request: Create concept 28, "Three Windows." Make one compact horizontal solid-black rounded rectangle the only emblem. Divide it into exactly three adjoining panels using exactly two broad white vertical gaps. Carve one bold, simple white vehicle silhouette from each panel: a side-view car with two clearly visible wheels in the left panel, a small boat with a distinct hull and one attached triangular sail in the center panel, and a clearly recognizable airplane with a fuselage, broad wings, and tail in the right panel. Keep the three icons separate, equal in optical weight, and readable at small size. Treat the white cutouts as a playful, sturdy stencil mark, not as interface buttons.
Brand context: NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES makes NEKOMO, portable toys for children. Its mission line is "Wonder to Go." The mark must make car, boat, and airplane recognizable.
Composition: White landscape canvas, 3:2 aspect ratio, generous clear margins. Center the single three-panel black emblem above the exact company name "NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES" in restrained uppercase sans-serif. Put the exact tagline "Wonder to Go." below it in smaller type.
Color and style: Pure solid black #000000 and white #FFFFFF only. Flat professional logo artwork with clean vector-like edges and broad, simple forms.
Constraints: The emblem contains only the one rounded black tile, two broad white dividing gaps, and three white vehicle cutouts. No panel borders, shadows, tiny details, windows, rigging, portholes, waves, extra symbols, or decoration. No additional text, misspellings, color, gradient, texture, 3D, mockup, or watermark. Do not imitate an existing brand.
```

最終プロンプト:

```text

Asset type: original company logo concept, vector-friendly

Let the continuous outline connect the vehicles without turning into a route map; preserve open white space and make the boundaries between car, boat, and airplane unmistakable. Use one consistent thick rounded stroke and only the essential contours, with fewer than ten major strokes or loops.
Brand context: NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES makes NEKOMO, portable toys for children. Its mission line is "Wonder to Go." The mark must make car, boat, and airplane recognizable.
Composition: White landscape canvas, 3:2 aspect ratio, generous clear margins. Center the connected black monoline emblem above the exact company name "NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES" in restrained uppercase sans-serif. Put the exact tagline "Wonder to Go." below it in smaller type.
Color and style: Pure solid black #000000 and white #FFFFFF only. Flat professional logo artwork with clean vector-like edges, balanced proportions, and generous white space.
Constraints: No decorative trails, arrows, stars, dashed lines, extra loops beyond the two necessary car wheels, tiny windows, rigging, portholes, waves, enclosure, or extra details.
