# NEKOMO boat logo concepts 21–22

Product context: NEKOMO, children’s portable toys.
Monochrome: solid black logo forms and type on pure white; flat, vector-friendly artwork.
Visible lockup requested for both: NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES / Wonder to Go.

## 21 — Cat Sail / ねこの帆船

Intent: an immediately recognizable friendly toy sailboat, with two short cat-ear-like sails as a secondary reading; one broad curved hull anchors the boat silhouette.

Final prompt:
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original NEKOMO company and product logo concept
Primary request: Create proposal 21, “Cat Sail / ねこの帆船”, for a child’s portable toy brand. The boat itself must be the logo and must read instantly as a friendly toy sailboat, before the subtle cat-ear idea.
Style/medium: premium minimalist flat logo, crisp vector-friendly geometry, solid black shapes only on pure white.
Composition/framing: centered 3:2 landscape layout with generous clear margins. Arrange one bold boat icon above two centered text lines.
Subject: a compact, broad, friendly sailboat silhouette made from 3 or 4 bold connected or solid simple forms: one curved, substantial hull and exactly two short triangular sails subtly shaped like cat ears, divided by a narrow clean gap or short mast. Keep the broad hull unmistakably boat-like at small size.
Text (verbatim): “NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES” and, smaller beneath it, “Wonder to Go.”
Constraints: preserve the exact spelling, capitalization, spacing, and period in both text lines. Keep the boat dominant and recognizable. The cat reading is secondary and comes only from the sail shapes.
Avoid: abstract symbol, tiny boat appended to a circle or monogram, face, eyes, whiskers, enclosure, gear, anchor, star, ropes, rigging, tiny details, multiple vehicles, clipart, imitation of an existing logo, mockup, 3D, gradients, shadows, textures, additional text or color.

## 22 — One-Fold Boat / ひと折りの舟

Intent: an instantly recognizable origami paper boat built from three broad black facets and clear white fold gaps, with a slightly lifted bow.

Final prompt:
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original NEKOMO company and product logo concept
Primary request: Create proposal 22, “One-Fold Boat / ひと折りの舟”, for a child’s portable toy brand. The icon must instantly read as an original origami paper boat, not as an abstract triangle or mountain.
Style/medium: premium minimalist flat logo, crisp vector-friendly geometry, black on pure white only.
Composition/framing: centered 3:2 landscape layout with generous clear margins. Arrange one bold boat icon above two centered text lines.
Subject: a wide, shallow, instantly recognizable paper boat hull with pointed folded corners and one central triangular paper fold; use only 3 broad black facets and a few clear white fold gaps. Give the bow a slight asymmetric lift suggesting departure while retaining the paper-boat silhouette.
Text (verbatim): “NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES” and, smaller beneath it, “Wonder to Go.”
Constraints: preserve exact spelling, capitalization, spacing, and period. Keep the boat recognizable when small; keep the paper-fold construction clear and elegant.
Avoid: mast, rigging, cat, eyes, waterlines, stars, arrows, enclosure, abstract triangle or mountain, multiple vehicles, clipart, imitation of an existing logo, mockup, 3D, gradients, shadows, textures, additional text or color.
