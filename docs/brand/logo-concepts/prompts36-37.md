# Logo concepts 36-37

## 36. Cat face boat

Intent: A shallow boat hull forms the lower half of a cat face. Two sails also read as ears, while two white eye cutouts make the cat explicit.

Prompt:
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original flat black-and-white toy-workshop logo concept
Primary request: Create one coherent mark that reads immediately as both a friendly cat and a sailboat. A large rounded, shallow boat hull also forms the lower half of a cat face. Cut two tiny clean white oval eyes into the black hull. Above it, place two short, broad triangular black sails that double as cat ears, with clear white gaps between both sails and the hull.
Style/medium: Original professional flat black shapes on pure white, vector-friendly, rounded toy proportions. Clean geometric sans-serif typography.
Composition/framing: Centered logo lockup on a 3:2 landscape canvas with generous even margins. Place the icon above the typography.
Text (verbatim): "NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES" with the smaller tagline "Wonder to Go." underneath. Use only these two lines, spelled and punctuated exactly.
Constraints: Keep the icon to three black shapes only, one hull and two sails/ears, plus the two white eye cutouts. The wordmark and tagline are the only text.
Avoid: A cat sticker beside a generic boat; cars, airplanes, or any vehicle except the sailboat; mouth, whiskers, nose, mast details, tail, star, ropes, rigging, waves, enclosing circle or frame; extra letters or words; product-name line; watermark; 3D, mockup, gradients, texture, or shadow.

## 37. Tail hull

Intent: One cat silhouette forms the boat hull with its curled tail, and a single sail rises from the same integrated shape.

Prompt:
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original flat black-and-white toy-workshop logo concept
Primary request: Create one coherent mark that reads immediately as both a friendly cat and a sailboat. Use a single flowing black cat profile: a small recognizable head with two ears at a raised bow, a smooth back and body sweeping into an elongated boat form, and a long curled tail that forms the shallow hull underneath. Add one bold, simple triangular black sail rising from the cat's back, touching the hull at its base so it reads clearly as a sail within the same boat silhouette.
Style/medium: Original professional flat black silhouette on pure white, vector-friendly, friendly rounded but bold toy proportions. Clean geometric sans-serif typography.
Composition/framing: Centered logo lockup on a 3:2 landscape canvas with generous even margins. Place the icon above the typography.
Text (verbatim): "NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES" with the smaller tagline "Wonder to Go." underneath. Use only these two lines, spelled and punctuated exactly.
Constraints: Use two black icon elements only, the single cat-and-hull silhouette and one sail. Keep the cat recognizable from its silhouette alone. The wordmark and tagline are the only text.
Avoid: A cat sticker beside a generic boat; cars, airplanes, or any vehicle except the sailboat; legs, paws, facial details, whiskers, rope, windows, waves, enclosing frame, extra symbols or text, product-name line, watermark, 3D, mockup, texture, gradients, or shadow.
