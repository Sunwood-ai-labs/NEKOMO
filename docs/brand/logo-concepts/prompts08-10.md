# Logo concepts 08–10

各案は NEKOMO の「どこでも持ち運んで、安全に遊ぶおもちゃ」という目的を、従来案と異なる意匠で表す。社名は `NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES`、タグラインは `Wonder to Go.` とする。

## 08 Door to Adventure

意図: 小さな開いた扉から、日常の場所が冒険へつながる様子を表す。
色: aubergine purple、warm apricot cream。

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: standalone brand logo concept, one logo only
Primary request: Create concept 08, “Door to Adventure,” for NEKOMO. Show a small open doorway or portal with a compact curved arch. Inside the doorway, reveal a minimal sun and distant hill or horizon. Add one tiny star outside the doorway to suggest an ordinary place opening into adventure. Use surprising negative space and refined paper-cut-like flat geometry. The identity should feel like a sophisticated children's imagination brand, not a travel agency.
Scene/backdrop: clean white or warm ivory, generous margins.
Subject: one iconic doorway emblem with a horizontal wordmark lockup.
Style/medium: premium, precise, minimal flat vector-friendly logo design.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2 canvas; centered, balanced lockup, crisp clear edges, generous white space.
Color palette: aubergine purple and warm apricot cream only.
Text (verbatim): “NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES” in exact uppercase spelling; subordinate tagline “Wonder to Go.” with exact capitalization and period.
Constraints: no literal cats, no planet orbit, no route arrow, no gear, no shield, no shadows, no gradients, no 3D. Make all text fully visible, spelled exactly, and cleanly typeset. One logo only.
```

## 09 Hands Full of Wonder

意図: 子どもの手の中にある喜びを、花のような光と受け止める手で表す。
色: warm coral red、warm sand、charcoal。

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: standalone brand logo concept, one logo only
Primary request: Create concept 09, “Hands Full of Wonder,” for NEKOMO. Form an emotional, caring logo from two broad rounded cupped hands, or one simplified bowl-shaped palm, holding one bold bright spark shaped like a playful flower. It should communicate joy held in a child's hand. Use iconic minimal soft organic silhouettes.
Scene/backdrop: clean white, generous margins.
Subject: one hand-and-spark emblem with a horizontal wordmark lockup.
Style/medium: original premium flat logo design, precise and vector-friendly.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2 canvas; centered, balanced lockup, crisp edges, generous white space.
Color palette: warm coral red, warm sand, and charcoal.
Text (verbatim): “NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES” in exact uppercase spelling; tagline “Wonder to Go.” with exact capitalization and period.
Constraints: no detailed fingers, no hearts, no safety certification seals, no cats, no gears, no pocket, no photorealism, no shadows, no gradients, no 3D. Make all text fully visible, spelled exactly, and cleanly typeset. One logo only.
```

## 10 Wonder in Motion

意図: W と小さな旗を兼ねるリボン形で、Wonder の動きを表す。
色: electric violet、lemon yellow、white。

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: standalone brand logo concept, one logo only
Primary request: Create concept 10, “Wonder in Motion,” a distinctive typographic identity for NEKOMO. Design a bespoke expressive W-shaped ribbon made from one bold folded flat shape. It should also suggest a small fluttering pennant and the letter W for Wonder. Keep it asymmetric and energetic while remaining legible. Pair it with a confident editorial lowercase wordmark.
Scene/backdrop: clean white, generous margins.
Subject: one W-ribbon/pennant emblem and exact lowercase wordmark lockup.
Style/medium: premium, precise flat vector-friendly typography and mark.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2 canvas; centered, balanced lockup, crisp edges, generous white space.
Color palette: electric violet and lemon yellow on white.
Text (verbatim): “nekoneko” in exact lowercase spelling, with small uppercase “INDUSTRIES”; secondary tagline “Wonder to Go.” with exact capitalization and period.
Constraints: no modular toy blocks, no gear, no shield, no cat silhouette, no orbit, no route line, no mockup, no shadows, no gradients, no 3D. Make all text fully visible, spelled exactly, and cleanly typeset. One logo only.
```
