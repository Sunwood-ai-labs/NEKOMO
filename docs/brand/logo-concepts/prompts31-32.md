# ロゴ案31〜32

生成方法: 内蔵の画像生成ツールで各案を個別に生成。参照画像は使用していない。

共通意図: NEKONEKO INDUSTRIESが作る、携帯できる子ども向け玩具NEKOMOのロゴ案。帆船だけを主役にし、乗り物の組み合わせにしない。

共通表記: 会社名は `NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES`、ミッションは `Wonder to Go.` と正確に記す。白地の横3:2、広い余白、黒一色のフラットなロゴにする。

## 31. Little Tallship Fleet / 二本マストの船団

意図: 二本マストと四枚の四角い帆を持つ帆船だけを大きく描き、帆船の印象を強める。

最終プロンプト:

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original logo concept for NEKOMO, a children’s portable toy brand from NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES.
Primary request: Create concept 31, “Little Tallship Fleet” (二本マストの船団). Show one unmistakable classic SAILING SHIP as the complete emblem, with the ship as the only visual subject.
Subject: One compact, friendly classic tallship with a shallow playful hull, exactly TWO simple upright masts, and exactly FOUR broad square or softly rounded rectangular sails, two on each mast. Make the sails the largest, clearest parts of the silhouette. Keep the hull broad and simple. Use only the two masts, four sails, and one hull to form a recognizable sailing ship.
Style/medium: Original professional toy-brand logo, simple vector-friendly geometry, bold soft shapes, solid flat black with clean white gaps.
Composition/framing: Center one large sailship emblem above the exact company name “NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES” and the smaller exact mission line “Wonder to Go.” Put only these two text lines beneath the emblem. White 3:2 landscape canvas with generous clear margins.
Color palette: Pure black #000000 on pure white #FFFFFF.
Constraints: Make the ship instantly recognizable as a sailing ship at thumbnail size. Use exactly two masts and four square sails. Keep the mark bold, simple, balanced, and easy to reproduce as a vector.
Avoid: Any other subject, extra vehicle or symbol, rope, rigging, pennants, portholes, stars, faces, windows, waves, extra ornaments, tiny details, frames, extra text, misspellings, gradients, shadows, texture, 3D, photography, mockups, watermark, or imitation of an existing logo.
```
