# 18〜20 ロゴ案の生成プロンプト

生成方法: 内蔵の画像生成ツールで各案を個別に生成。参照画像は使用していない。

共通意図: どこへでも持ち歩ける子ども向け玩具を作る会社のロゴ。AIは裏側の方法として扱う。

共通仕様: 横3:2、余白のある白地、黒いマーク、社名 `NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES`、タグライン `Wonder to Go.`。フラットな白黒表現。

## 18 Rolling go. / 小さなgo.

意図: 読みやすい短い「go.」を、出発を表す独自の文字記号としてまとめる。

最終プロンプト:

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original company logo concept, vector-friendly
Primary request: Create concept 18, "Rolling go." The large central mark is a bespoke lowercase typographic symbol reading exactly "go." and nothing else. Use a bold rounded custom single-storey g and o. Give the g a short asymmetrical curved descender that lightly suggests rolling forward, make the o nearly circular with one subtle off-center counter, and lift the period slightly for a playful departure. It must immediately read as the three characters g, o, period, not as abstract symbols.
Brand context: The company aims to make toys that children can carry anywhere. AI is only a behind-the-scenes method. Suggest a small sense of wonder and readiness to go without depicting a child, toy, or AI.
Composition: White landscape canvas with a 3:2 aspect ratio, very generous clear margins. Center one cohesive three-character black mark. Beneath it, center the exact company name "NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES" in restrained uppercase sans-serif. On the next line, set the exact tagline "Wonder to Go." smaller. Keep all text crisp, correctly spelled, and separated from the mark.
Color and style: Pure solid black #000000 on pure white #FFFFFF only. Flat, professional, original logo artwork with clean vector-like edges.
Constraints: Keep the mark itself only "go.". No arrows, stars, swooshes, wheels, ribbons, colors, shadows, gradients, textures, 3D effects, mockups, watermarks, or extra text. Do not imitate or resemble an existing brand mark.
```
