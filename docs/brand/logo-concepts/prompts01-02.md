# ロゴ案01・02の生成プロンプト

企業名は「ネコネコインダストリー / NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES」、製品ブランドは「NEKOMO」として扱う。これは企業ロゴのコンセプト提案であり、確定ロゴではない。

## 01 Workshop

- 狙い: 角張ったNに猫の頭を組み込み、工房の確かさと手仕事感を表す。
- 色: チャコール `#292929`、銅色 `#B87333`、アイボリー背景。
- 用途: NEKONEKO INDUSTRIESの企業ロゴ案。自宅で3Dプリントする乗り物玩具と、猫のAI職人が働く工場を想定。
- 最終プロンプト:

```text
Logo-brand concept, original proposal for NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES, a Japanese maker of NEKOMO vehicle toys designed for home 3D printing, with AI artisan cats in the workshop. Create one refined corporate logo lockup on a warm white or ivory background, landscape canvas about 3:2 with generous clear margins, centered and standalone. The emblem is a bespoke cat head integrated into a squared uppercase N monogram. Use sturdy flat geometric silhouettes, balanced negative space, and a strong simple shape that stays legible at small size. Give the mark a mature, dependable craft-workshop character. Palette limited to charcoal #292929 and copper #B87333 on ivory. Include the exact uppercase wordmark "NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES", spelled precisely, in clean highly legible typography below or beside the emblem. Flat vector-friendly logo aesthetic. Do not add the Japanese company name. No generic gear icon, no extra icons, no literal vehicles, no mascot illustration, no photo or mockup, no gradients, no shadows, no watermark, no border, no extra words. Do not imitate any real brand.
```

## 02 Navigation

- 狙い: 地平線・波・軌道・航路を思わせる一本線に、猫耳の頂点を控えめに含め、移動領域の広がりを抽象的に示す。
- 色: ネイビー `#18324B`、ティール `#1E9D9A`、白背景。
- 用途: NEKONEKO INDUSTRIESの企業ロゴ案。陸・海・空・水中へ広がる乗り物構想を、乗り物の絵を使わず表す。
- 最終プロンプト:

```text
Logo-brand concept, original proposal for NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES. Create one minimal navigation emblem and horizontal corporate logo lockup on a clean white background, landscape canvas about 3:2 with generous clear margins. The emblem uses one continuous, elegant route-like line to suggest a horizon, wave, orbit, and direction through space. Work two subtle pointed cat-ear peaks into the path, without drawing a cat face. Let the emblem evoke travel across land, sea, air, and underwater through abstract spatial direction only. Use precise thin-to-medium geometry that remains legible at small size. Palette limited to navy #18324B and teal #1E9D9A. Place an uppercase horizontal wordmark beside the emblem in a clean humanist sans serif. Render the exact text "NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES", spelled precisely, with high legibility. Flat vector-friendly, professional and understated. No cat-head illustration, no N monogram, no industrial block mark, no gear, no literal vehicle drawings, no mockup or photograph, no gradients, no shadows, no watermark, no border, no extra words. Do not imitate any real brand.
```
