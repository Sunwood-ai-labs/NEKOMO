# Logo concepts 11 and 12

## 11 Rolling Wonder

狙い: 星が車輪に乗って前へ進み、わくわくが出発する印象を表す。
色: 明るい朱色、深いネイビー。

最終プロンプト:

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: standalone corporate logo concept for the NEKOMO product company
Primary request: Create an original, premium, playful identity that makes “Wonder to Go.” feel like a small burst of wonder setting off into the world. The company’s goal is portable toys children can carry and safely play with anywhere; this is the intended feeling, not a claim to print.
Scene/backdrop: clean white background.
Subject: One iconic four-point spark-star riding on two small rounded wheels, like a tiny toy rolling enthusiastically to the right. The star is the main symbol. Add one short offset motion cut or gap to communicate forward motion.
Style/medium: Minimal smooth flat logo shapes, broad clean geometry, friendly geometric sans-serif lettering.
Composition/framing: Standalone horizontal lockup, centered with generous whitespace, 3:2 landscape.
Color palette: Bright vermilion star with deep navy wheels and lettering.
Text (verbatim): “NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES” and “Wonder to Go.” Use only these two text strings. Render every letter exactly, clearly, and without extra text.
Constraints: Original, polished logo on white. Keep the mark simple and legible at small size.
Avoid: Cats, gears, shields, bags, a detailed vehicle, generic automotive racing badge, airline marks, mockups, scenes, photos, 3D, shadows, gradients, textures, watermarks, additional text.
```

## 12 Folded Adventure

狙い: 紙飛行機が星を運びながら前上方へ飛び、手作りのおもちゃが想像力を外へ連れ出す印象を表す。
色: スカイブルー、サニーライム、濃いインク色。

最終プロンプト:

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: standalone corporate logo concept for the NEKOMO product company
Primary request: Create an original, premium, playful identity that makes “Wonder to Go.” feel like imagination being carried out into the world. The company’s goal is portable toys children can carry and safely play with anywhere; this is the intended feeling, not a claim to print.
Scene/backdrop: clean white background.
Subject: A beautifully simple paper airplane with a tiny four-point star cut out of one wing. The folded paper shape points forward and slightly upward, suggesting a handmade toy carrying imagination outward.
Style/medium: Crisp broad flat geometric shapes with friendly rounded corners; clean professional logo lettering.
Composition/framing: Standalone horizontal lockup, centered with generous whitespace, 3:2 landscape.
Color palette: Sky blue with sunny lime and dark ink contrast.
Text (verbatim): “NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES” and “Wonder to Go.” Use only these two text strings. Render every letter exactly, clearly, and without extra text.
Constraints: Make the paper plane and the rolling star idea in other concepts clearly distinguishable; use no orbit or route line.
Avoid: Airline swoosh, route line, orbit, cats, pockets, shields, bags, mockups, scenes, photos, 3D, shadows, gradients, textures, watermarks, additional text.
```
