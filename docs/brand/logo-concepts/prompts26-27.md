# NEKOMO logo concepts 26–27

## 26. All-Terrain Wonder（陸海空のおもちゃ）

意図: 浅い船体、下の二輪、船体の上にある飛行機の水平翼と短い尾翼を一体の玩具にまとめ、車・船・飛行機を同時に伝える。

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Original logo concept for NEKOMO, a children’s portable toy brand from NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES.
Primary request: Create a simple, original logo concept titled “All-Terrain Wonder” (陸海空のおもちゃ), for “Wonder to Go.” The mark is ONE unified, charming, compact imaginary toy vehicle that immediately and separately reads as a car, a boat, and an airplane.
Subject: Combine one obvious shallow BOAT HULL, TWO ROUND CAR WHEELS clearly beneath it, and an unmistakable small AIRPLANE with a real horizontal wing silhouette and short tail profile above the hull. The wing must not look like a triangular sail. No mast. Make all three vehicle readings clear at first glance, not boat-only and not a sailboat on wheels.
Style/medium: Flat-vector-friendly professional logo, with 5–6 bold black main shapes, clean white gaps, soft rounded original forms, carefully balanced proportions.
Composition/framing: Center the compact emblem above the exact supporting text “NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES” and the smaller exact line “Wonder to Go.” Landscape 3:2 canvas with generous whitespace and margins.
Color palette: Pure black shapes on pure white.
Constraints: One unified toy vehicle, no collage. Preserve the exact supporting text and capitalization.
Avoid: Stars, faces, extra ornaments, windows, waves, frames, roof mast, triangular sail, gradients, shadows, texture, 3D, photography, mockups, imitation of existing logos, and any extra text.
```

## 27. Three Toys, One Family（ならぶ乗り物）

意図: 車・船・飛行機の横顔を同じ形と太さで一列に並べ、各乗り物を見分けやすくしながら一つのブランドマークにまとめる。

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Original logo concept for NEKOMO, a children’s portable toy brand from NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES.
Primary request: Create a simple, original, refined and friendly logo concept titled “Three Toys, One Family” (ならぶ乗り物), for “Wonder to Go.”
Subject: One compact horizontal emblem made of exactly THREE tiny, unmistakable side-profile vehicle silhouettes: left, a small rounded CAR with two wheels; center, a BOAT with a clear hull and one simple sail or cabin; right, an AIRPLANE with a clear fuselage, actual horizontal wing, and tail. All three are separate and individually recognizable at thumbnail size.
Style/medium: Professional flat-vector-friendly toy brand mark. Use the same simple geometric language and equal optical weight for all three icons.
Composition/framing: Align the three silhouettes as one compact, carefully spaced group above the exact supporting text “NEKONEKO INDUSTRIES” and smaller exact line “Wonder to Go.” Landscape 3:2 canvas with generous whitespace.
Color palette: Pure black on pure white.
Constraints: Reduce each icon to its essentials. The car has no tiny windows, the boat has no rigging or waves, and the airplane has no portholes. Keep alignment and spacing short and intentional. No containers, lines, arrows, or background art.
Avoid: Indistinct blobs, stars, faces, ornaments, extra detail, gradients, shadows, texture, 3D, photography, mockups, imitation of existing logos, and any extra text.
```
